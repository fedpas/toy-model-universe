# Update: step 12 (a spring on the cube), the Map page, and the credit to Claude

**Step 12, "A spring on the cube: vertices, edges, faces and the rotor"** (equations page, after step 11)
New: SpringSection.jsx, SpringWidgets.jsx (five live widgets), springEngine.js, springCopy.js (EN/IT), springData.js, springSelfcheckSource.js, selfcheck/spring_selfcheck.py, selfcheck/spring.json, selfcheck/regen_spring.sh.
Content: a unit mass on every vertex of the n-cube, a Hooke spring on every edge, one scalar displacement each. Vertices = masses (x'' = -Lx, L = n I - adjacency, Walsh normal modes with omega^2 = 2j and multiplicity C(n,j)), edges = springs (energy 1/2 sum of stretches^2, L = d1 d1^T), faces = closure (d1 d2 = 0, b1 = 0, the stretch of a displacement sums to zero round every face, an arbitrary edge field does not). One oscillator is the rotor flow s' = omega B s with B = uv: B^2 = -1, +1, 0 give oscillator, inverted oscillator, free motion (the three kinds of i of step 11); the sandwich needs the half angle. Clifford network: D = sum e_i (left multiplications), D^2 = nI, flux -1 (pi) through every face, K = nI - D is a genuine edge energy sum (x_i - s_ij x_j)^2 and has two levels n -+ sqrt(n), each 2^(n-1) times. The labels of step 11 are the Walsh characters.
Checks: `python3 selfcheck/spring_selfcheck.py` (60 exact claims in 22 rows, standard library, three negative controls), `--row B5`, `--compare selfcheck/spring.json`; `sh selfcheck/regen_spring.sh` rebuilds spring.json, springData.js, springSelfcheckSource.js.
Status tags: all 60 claims checked; Hooke's law, the hypercube spectrum, strain compatibility and the signed matrix (Huang 2019) are standard; ours are the arrangement, the flux-pi reading of the signs and the label = mode reading. Open: vector displacements, rigid body, action principle, continuum limit, damping, any link between the signed network and the Dirac operator on the demicube. 1, 3, 3, 1 at n = 3 are binomials, not generations.
